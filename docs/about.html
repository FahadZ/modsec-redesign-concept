Who we are

Local security, built on service

Modern Security Systems has served the Cambridge, Kitchener and Waterloo community since 1975, building a reputation on quality service and quality products.

Today we protect homes, businesses and industrial sites across Cambridge, Kitchener, Waterloo and Guelph. Every system is backed by ULC-certified monitoring centres across Canada, and as a Honeywell dealer we install technology designed to work together.

Customers have dealt directly with owner Rob Racine for years — when they have a question about their system or monitoring, they get a quick, professional answer from someone who knows them.
