FAQ

Questions we hear every week

How much does a monitored security system cost?

It depends on the size of your property and the equipment you choose, which is why we start with a free security analysis and then give you a clear, itemised quote. [Owner: add a typical starting price range.]

What happens when my alarm goes off?

Trained operators at our ULC-certified monitoring centres receive the signal, work through your call list and dispatch the right responders. Monitoring is staffed 24/7 with centres across Canada for redundancy.

Do I still need a landline?

Not always. Many modern systems can communicate over cellular or internet connections. We’ll recommend the most reliable option for your property during your free analysis.

Can I check on my home or business when I’m away?

Yes. As a Honeywell dealer we install connected systems that work with the MAXPRO Mobile app, so you can check in, get alerts and stay in control from your phone.

Do you protect businesses and industrial sites?

Absolutely. From small local shops to large plants and warehouses, we design systems that detect intruders, fire, heat gain/loss, chemicals and more. Ask about commercial service agreements.

Will a monitored system lower my insurance?

Many insurers offer discounts for monitored systems. Every provider is different, so check with yours — we can supply the documentation they ask for.
